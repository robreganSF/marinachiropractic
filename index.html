A practice built around your schedule

After years running a clinic on Chestnut Street in San Francisco's Marina District, Dr. Neely rebuilt the practice around a simple observation: the hardest part of chiropractic care was never the adjustment. It was getting to the appointment.

Meet nearby

Appointments happen at convenient, easy-to-reach spots in your neighborhood — most patients walk or make a short drive.

A real treatment space

The van is a high-roof, extended-wheelbase Sprinter outfitted with a proper chiropractic table. Private, clean, and purpose-built.

One doctor, every visit

You see Dr. Neely herself each time. No rotating providers, no front desk, no being handed off.

Straightforward and unhurried

Appointment-only scheduling means visits start on time and you are not squeezed between two other patients.

What patients come in for

People of all ages seek chiropractic care for a wide range of complaints. Some of the most common include:

  • Low back pain
  • Neck pain
  • Mid and upper back pain
  • Headaches
  • Shoulder and arm pain
  • Sciatica and leg pain
  • Joint stiffness
  • Muscle spasms
  • Numbness and tingling
  • Posture concerns
  • Sports and training strain
  • Desk and commute strain

Not sure if chiropractic is right for what you are dealing with? Call and describe it — if it is outside her scope, Dr. Neely will tell you and point you somewhere useful.
