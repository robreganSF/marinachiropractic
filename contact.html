Where appointments happen

Marina Chiropractic is a mobile practice. Rather than travelling to a clinic, you meet Dr. Neely at an agreed spot near you, and treatment takes place in a fully equipped treatment van.

She regularly sees patients in:

  • Marina District
  • Cow Hollow
  • Pacific Heights
  • Sausalito
  • Mill Valley
  • Tiburon

When you book, Dr. Neely will confirm the exact meeting point, tell you where she will be parked, and describe the van so you can spot it easily.

Outside these areas? Ask anyway — the service area is flexible.
